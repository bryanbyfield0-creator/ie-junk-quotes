Redlands runs its own bulky pickup

The City of Redlands says single-family residential customers receive two free bulky-item pickups per year, with up to three items per collection. Other items can be collected for a fee as a "special haul." City crews can't go inside a building to collect items, so everything has to be at the curb.

Estate and downsizing cleanouts

For a full-house cleanout, ask providers whether they sort donations, how they handle personal papers and photos, and whether they'll sweep out when done. Get the scope in writing, especially if the home is being sold.

Paint, chemicals, motor oil, batteries, and other household hazardous waste can go to San Bernardino County Fire's free household hazardous waste (HHW) collection sites for county residents. See our hazardous waste guide.

Redlands FAQ

How many items can I put out for Redlands bulky pickup?

Up to three items per collection, with two free collections per year for single-family customers. More items can be picked up for a fee.

Will Redlands crews come inside to get furniture?

No. The city says crews can't enter a building. A private junk removal crew can carry items out.

How much does junk removal cost in Redlands?

Pricing mostly depends on how much truck space your items take, plus heavy materials, stairs or long carries, and disposal fees. Compare a few quotes from Redlands-area providers. See our cost factors guide.
