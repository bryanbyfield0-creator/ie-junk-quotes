Quarterly bulky pickups

The City of Yucaipa says residents can request up to four curbside bulky-item collections in a 12-month period, up to five items each, through Yucaipa Disposal (Burrtec). Pickups happen during the second week of February, May, August, and November, and the program also covers electronic waste. Yucaipa Disposal also rents 3-cubic-yard bins for cleanups.

Hot tubs, sheds, and big yard jobs

Spas and sheds need to be taken apart, which is beyond curbside pickup. See our hot tub removal guide. Get quotes from crews that handle demolition and haul-away together.

Paint, chemicals, motor oil, batteries, and other household hazardous waste can go to San Bernardino County Fire's free household hazardous waste (HHW) collection sites for county residents. See our hazardous waste guide.
