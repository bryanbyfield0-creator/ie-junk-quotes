How Inland Empire Junk Removal Quotes works

We're a small, independent referral service in the Inland Empire. We built this site so you can find someone to haul away junk without calling a dozen companies.

  1. You fill out the quote request form.
  2. We review it and share your job details and contact information with one or more independent junk removal providers who serve your area.
  3. Providers contact you directly to ask questions, look at photos, or schedule a quick estimate.
  4. You decide who to hire, if anyone. Your agreement is directly with that provider.

Getting accurate quotes

  • Describe the amount in plain terms: "a few items," "about a pickup truck load," "a full one-car garage."
  • Mention anything heavy (concrete, dirt, tile, a piano) or hard to reach (stairs, a long walk from the truck, a tight side yard).
  • Have photos ready. Many providers can quote from photos.

What we are, and what we aren't

  • We're not a junk removal company and we don't haul anything.
  • We don't guarantee any provider's work, price, insurance, or disposal practices.
  • We may receive a fee from providers for referrals. It never costs you anything.
  • We don't post fake reviews or ratings.

Get free junk removal quotes

Inland Empire Junk Removal Quotes is a free referral service. We are not a junk removal company: we don't haul anything, and we don't hold a contractor's license. When you send a request, we pass it to independent local junk removal providers who can contact you with quotes. Any provider you hire is solely responsible for its work, licensing, insurance, and how it disposes of your items. Ask for proof of insurance before hiring, and for demolition work (sheds, decks, built-in spas), check the contractor's license at cslb.ca.gov.
